How this was sorted and what to keep in mind

Each listing's full notice text was read, not just its title. A bid is drone related when the notice is about a drone or UAS purchase, drone parts or payloads, drone services such as remote pilots, R&D or grants for drones, or counter-UAS and robotics work. Mentions of drones as one topic among many (for example a seminar series) are kept out and noted.

  • The keyword profile matches on FSC 1560 and 1680, several NAICS codes and keywords such as aircraft, UAS, unmanned and drone. The FSC codes and the word "aircraft" in titles pull in a large volume of parts buys for manned aircraft.
  • Set-aside is recorded only where the notice states one. "Not stated" means the entry did not say, not that the bid is unrestricted.
  • Some notices are sole-source intents or market research, not open solicitations. The note on each card says which.
  • State and local entries come through third-party bid portals and may need registration or a fee for the full package.
  • Days left are counted from the snapshot date, not from today.
